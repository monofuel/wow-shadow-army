# Murist — the human seat's character canon

Facts monofuel has declared canonical for Murist (undead warlock, the
human seat on the home realm). Agents advising gear, professions, or
economy should honor these rather than optimizing from scratch.

- **Professions: Tailoring + Enchanting.** Canonical, per monofuel
  2026-08-15 — honoring his original WoW undead warlock **Raguri**
  (tailoring/enchanting, with **Fishing** as the highest skill).
  Fishing is therefore the canonical flavor secondary.
- Practical implications: cloth drops feed tailoring (bags, gear);
  greens feed disenchanting (the realm has no player economy, so
  self-crafted wands — Lesser Magic Wand at enchanting 10 — are the
  caster wand path); advisor/bags disposition logic should eventually
  treat cloth and disenchantable greens as KEEP-for-profession, not
  vendor fodder.

- **Murist's build is the anchor for bot professions.** As seats
  mature, each bot's professions get decided relative to this anchor —
  complementary coverage (e.g. the warrior seat taking mining/smithing,
  a priest taking herbalism/alchemy) rather than everyone cloning the
  caster pairing. No assignments are canon yet; decide as progress
  demands and record them here.

advisor does with this.
